35 Top Tier Tech Engineering Blogs (Real-World Architectures) ​

How industry leaders like Netflix, Uber, Meta, Discord, Slack, and Stripe build, scale, and evolve distributed production architectures.

💡 How to Read Engineering Blogs for System Design Interviews

Real-world architectures at top tech companies are never designed in a vacuum—they evolve through traffic surges, cascading outages, and technical debt. In senior and staff engineering interviews, citing real-world examples (e.g. "Similar to Uber's H3 hexagonal hierarchical spatial index for dispatch" or "Discord's migration of trillions of messages from Cassandra to ScyllaDB") immediately establishes engineering credibility.

Focus on 3 types of articles:

  1. Outage Post-Mortems: Study cascade failures and fallbacks (e.g. Cloudflare, Slack);
  2. Architecture Migrations: Learn how teams shard and replace data stores with zero downtime;
  3. Core Production Primitives: Learn how Stripe handles API idempotency or how Figma implements CRDTs for collaborative editing.
