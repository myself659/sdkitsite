36 Curated YouTube System Design Playlists & Lectures ​

A structured catalog of top video playlists and deep dives from MIT, CMU, and premier engineering leaders.

💡 Learning Strategy: Structured Playlists > Fragmented Clips

When learning distributed systems through video, it is easy to accumulate shallow familiarity without architectural mastery. Short 10-minute clips demonstrate how things look, but rarely explain why.

Recommended Learning Framework:

  1. Anchor in Core Lecture Series: Build your mental model using Martin Kleppmann’s Distributed Systems course and MIT 6.824 for fundamental consensus, RPC, and replication trade-offs.
  2. Close the Video and Whiteboard: After analyzing a case study (such as Uber or Swiggy), draw the entire system from scratch—identifying SPOFs and latency bottlenecks.
  3. Use Focused Deep Dives for Gaps: Target specific weaknesses (such as the SAGA pattern, Blue/Green deployments, or WebSockets) with dedicated TechPrimers walkthroughs.
