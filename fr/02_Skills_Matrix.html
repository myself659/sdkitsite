Matrice de 99 Compétences Clés en Conception de Systèmes ​

Total des Éléments: 99

#CatégorieConcept / CompétenceNiveauPourquoi c'est importantForme d'évaluation en entretien
1FundamentalsScalability: vertical versus horizontalFoundationThe first fork in every design discussion.'This works on one server. Now handle 10x traffic.'
2FundamentalsLatency versus throughputFoundationTwo different problems that beginners conflate constantly.'Do you optimise for latency or throughput here? Why?'
3FundamentalsAvailability, reliability and durabilityFoundationThree distinct guarantees with different costs.'What does five nines actually cost you?'
4FundamentalsCAP theorem and PACELCCoreThe framing behind every distributed database choice.'Is this system CP or AP? Justify it.'
5FundamentalsConsistency models (strong, eventual, causal)CoreDetermines what your users can observe.'A user posts and does not see it. Is that acceptable here?'
6FundamentalsBack-of-the-envelope estimationFoundationThe first five minutes of every design interview.'How much storage does this need per year?'
7FundamentalsFunctional versus non-functional requirementsFoundationClarifying these first is the single highest-scoring habit.Watching whether you gather requirements before designing.
8FundamentalsSingle points of failureCoreEvery box you draw is a thing that can fail.'What happens when this component dies?'
9FundamentalsTrade-off thinkingCoreThere is no correct design, only justified ones.The whole interview is an assessment of this.
10NetworkingDNS and how requests are routedFoundationThe entry point of every system you design.'Walk me through what happens before the request reaches you.'
11NetworkingHTTP, HTTPS and TLSFoundationThe protocol layer of almost every design.'Where does TLS terminate in your architecture?'
12NetworkingTCP versus UDPCoreVideo, gaming and telemetry make different choices.'Why would you use UDP for this?'
13NetworkingWebSockets, long polling and Server-Sent EventsCoreEvery real-time feature needs one of these.'How does the client learn about a new message?'
14NetworkinggRPC and protocol buffersCoreInternal service communication at scale.'REST or gRPC between these services?'
15NetworkingContent Delivery NetworksCoreStatic assets, video and global latency.'Users in Australia see 3 second loads. Fix it.'
16NetworkingProxies: forward, reverse and API gatewayCoreThe layer where auth, routing and rate limiting live.'Where does authentication happen in this design?'
17Load BalancingLoad balancer types and placementFoundationAppears in essentially every design you will draw.'Where do the load balancers go, and how many?'
18Load BalancingAlgorithms (round robin, least connections, hashing)CoreThe choice affects cache hit rates and fairness.'Why consistent hashing here rather than round robin?'
19Load BalancingLayer 4 versus Layer 7 load balancingCoreTransport versus application-aware routing.'What can an L7 balancer do that L4 cannot?'
20Load BalancingHealth checks and failoverCoreHow the system removes a dead node automatically.'How does traffic stop going to a failed server?'
21Load BalancingSticky sessions and why to avoid themCoreStatelessness is what makes horizontal scaling work.'Why is holding session state in memory a problem?'
22CachingCache strategies (cache-aside, write-through, write-back)CoreThe choice determines your consistency and failure behaviour.'Which caching strategy, and what breaks with each?'
23CachingCache eviction policies (LRU, LFU, TTL)CoreWhat leaves the cache when it is full.'Design an LRU cache.' Also a common coding question.
24CachingCache invalidationAdvancedFamously one of the two hard problems in computing.'The data changed. How does the cache find out?'
25CachingRedis and MemcachedCoreThe two standard distributed cache choices.'Redis or Memcached here? What is the difference?'
26CachingMulti-level caching (browser, CDN, application, database)CoreCaching happens at four layers, not one.'Where would you cache in this design?'
27CachingThundering herd and cache stampedeAdvancedWhat happens when a hot key expires under load.'The cache expires and the database falls over. Prevent it.'
28DatabasesSQL versus NoSQL selectionFoundationThe most common design question after scaling.'Postgres or Cassandra here? Defend it.'
29DatabasesRelational modelling and normalisationFoundationThe schema is the most expensive thing to change later.'Design the schema for this system.'
30DatabasesIndexing and query performanceCoreThe difference between 5ms and 5 seconds.'This query is slow at scale. What do you do?'
31DatabasesReplication (leader-follower, multi-leader, leaderless)CoreRead scaling and availability.'How do you scale reads? What breaks?'
32DatabasesReplication lag and read-your-writesAdvancedThe user who cannot see their own comment.'A user updates their profile and sees the old one. Why?'
33DatabasesSharding and partitioning strategiesCoreThe main technique for scaling writes.'How do you shard this? What is your partition key?'
34DatabasesConsistent hashingAdvancedAdding and removing nodes without reshuffling everything.'How do you add a shard without moving all the data?'
35DatabasesHot partitions and skewAdvancedOne celebrity user destroys a naive sharding scheme.'Your partition key is user ID. What about the top 1% of users?'
36DatabasesACID transactions and isolation levelsCoreMoney and inventory correctness.'Two users buy the last item. What happens?'
37DatabasesDistributed transactions and two-phase commitAdvancedWhy microservices avoid them.'How do you keep two services consistent without 2PC?'
38DatabasesSaga pattern and compensating transactionsAdvancedThe practical answer to distributed consistency.'Order placed, payment failed. Now what?'
39DatabasesNoSQL types (key-value, document, wide-column, graph)CoreFour families with genuinely different use cases.'Which NoSQL family fits this access pattern?'
40DatabasesTime-series and analytical databasesAdvancedMetrics, logging and OLAP workloads.'Where do you store 2 billion events per day?'
41DatabasesDatabase connection poolingCoreA classic production failure at scale.'Your service scales up and the database refuses connections.'
42StorageObject storage versus block versus fileCoreImages, video and backups all live in object storage.'Where do the uploaded videos go?'
43StorageBlob storage and presigned URLsCoreUploading without routing bytes through your servers.'A user uploads a 2GB file. Design the path.'
44StorageData retention, archival and tieringAdvancedStorage cost management at scale.'This is 40TB per month. What does it cost in year three?'
45MessagingMessage queues and why they existCoreDecoupling, buffering and absorbing spikes.'The email send slows checkout. Fix it.'
46MessagingKafka and log-based streamingCoreThe default event backbone at scale.'Explain partitions and consumer groups.'
47MessagingRabbitMQ, SQS and traditional brokersCoreTask queues versus event streams.'Kafka or SQS here? What is the difference?'
48MessagingPub/sub versus point-to-pointCoreOne consumer or many.'Multiple services need this event. Design it.'
49MessagingDelivery guarantees (at-most, at-least, exactly-once)AdvancedExactly-once is harder than candidates think.'How do you achieve exactly-once end to end?'
50MessagingIdempotency and deduplicationCoreRetries are guaranteed; double-charging must not be.'The payment webhook fires twice. What must be true?'
51MessagingDead letter queues and poison messagesCoreThe message that fails forever.'A message keeps failing. What happens to it?'
52MessagingEvent-driven architecture and event sourcingAdvancedDecoupled systems and auditable state.'What does event sourcing buy you, and what does it cost?'
53ArchitectureMonolith versus microservicesCoreMost teams should start with a monolith. Saying so scores.'Should we split this? Usually no. Explain why.'
54ArchitectureService boundaries and domain-driven designAdvancedWhere to cut is the hard part, not how.'How would you split this system into services?'
55ArchitectureAPI design (REST, GraphQL, gRPC)CoreThe contract between every component you draw.'Design the API for this feature.'
56ArchitectureAPI gateway and backend for frontendCoreThe edge layer in a microservices design.'Twelve services and three clients. Design the entry point.'
57ArchitectureService discovery and service meshAdvancedHow services find each other dynamically.'How does service A know where service B is?'
58ArchitectureStateless services and externalised stateCoreThe precondition for horizontal scaling.'Why must this service be stateless?'
59ArchitectureRead-heavy versus write-heavy designCoreThe ratio changes every decision you make.'This is 1000:1 read to write. What changes?'
60ArchitectureFan-out on read versus fan-out on writeAdvancedThe central trade-off in feed design.'Design a news feed. Which fan-out, and why?'
61ArchitectureMulti-region and geo-distributionAdvancedGlobal latency and disaster recovery.'Users in three continents. Design for it.'
62ArchitectureLambda and Kappa architecturesAdvancedBatch plus streaming, or streaming only.'Do you need a batch layer here?'
63ReliabilityRedundancy and failoverCoreEvery critical component needs a spare.'This database is a single point of failure. Fix it.'
64ReliabilityCircuit breakers, retries and exponential backoffCorePreventing a slow service from taking down everything.'The downstream service is slow. What does your user see?'
65ReliabilityTimeouts and bulkheadsCoreContaining failure rather than propagating it.'How do you stop one slow dependency exhausting your threads?'
66ReliabilityRate limiting and throttling algorithmsCoreProtecting cost, capacity and fairness.'Design a rate limiter.' A very common standalone question.
67ReliabilityGraceful degradation and load sheddingAdvancedServing something rather than failing entirely.'Traffic is 5x forecast. What do you turn off first?'
68ReliabilityBackpressureAdvancedTelling upstream to slow down rather than collapsing.'The consumer cannot keep up. What now?'
69ReliabilityDisaster recovery, RTO and RPOAdvancedHow much downtime and data loss is acceptable.'A region goes down. What is your recovery plan?'
70ReliabilityChaos engineeringAdvancedProving resilience rather than assuming it.'How do you know your failover actually works?'
71ObservabilityLogging, metrics and tracesCoreThe three pillars, and what each is for.'How do you debug a slow request across eight services?'
72ObservabilityDistributed tracing and correlation IDsCoreFollowing one request through a distributed system.'Where is the latency in this chain?'
73ObservabilitySLIs, SLOs and error budgetsAdvancedTurning reliability into a measurable commitment.'What is your SLO, and what happens when you miss it?'
74ObservabilityAlerting and on-call designCoreAlerting on symptoms, not causes.'What do you alert on, and what do you deliberately not?'
75ObservabilityHealth checks and readiness probesCoreHow orchestrators know a service is usable.'What is the difference between liveness and readiness?'
76SecurityAuthentication and authorisation at scaleCoreEvery system with users needs both.'How does a request get authenticated across services?'
77SecurityOAuth2, OIDC and JWT in system designCoreThe standard token flows.'Where is the token validated, and by whom?'
78SecurityEncryption in transit and at restCoreA baseline expectation and a compliance requirement.'How is this sensitive data protected?'
79SecuritySecrets managementCoreCredentials must not live in code or config files.'Where do the production credentials live?'
80SecurityDDoS protection and WAFAdvancedSurviving hostile traffic.'Someone floods your API. Respond.'
81SecurityData privacy, GDPR and DPDP in designAdvancedRegulation shapes architecture, especially data location.'A user requests deletion. Where is their data?'
82PerformanceCapacity planning and load testingCoreKnowing your ceiling before your users find it.'How many servers do you need? Show your working.'
83PerformanceConnection management and keep-aliveAdvancedConnection overhead at high request rates.'What is the cost of opening a connection per request?'
84PerformanceCompression and payload optimisationCoreBandwidth cost and mobile latency.'Users are on 3G. What do you change?'
85PerformanceBatching and request coalescingAdvancedReducing round trips and downstream load.'You are making 200 calls per page. Fix it.'
86PerformanceDatabase query optimisation at scaleCoreThe most common actual bottleneck.'Where is the bottleneck in this design?' Usually the database.
87Low Level DesignSOLID principlesCoreThe framing for every LLD code review.'Which principle does this design violate?'
88Low Level DesignCreational patterns (factory, builder, singleton)CoreObject construction problems.'Design a notification system with multiple channels.'
89Low Level DesignStructural patterns (adapter, decorator, facade)CoreComposing and wrapping behaviour.'Add logging to this without modifying it.'
90Low Level DesignBehavioural patterns (strategy, observer, state)CoreVarying behaviour at runtime.'Design a payment system supporting five providers.'
91Low Level DesignClass diagrams and UML basicsCoreThe output format of an LLD round.'Draw the classes and their relationships.'
92Low Level DesignConcurrency in object designAdvancedThread safety in shared components.'Is this design thread-safe? Make it so.'
93Low Level DesignCommon LLD problems (parking lot, elevator, BookMyShow)CoreAmazon and Indian product companies ask these directly.'Design a parking lot system.' Extremely common in India.
94PracticeStructuring the 45-minute interviewCoreRequirements, estimation, high level, deep dive, wrap up.Running out of time is the most common failure mode.
95PracticeDriving the conversationCoreThe candidate leads; the interviewer probes.Silence and waiting to be asked both score badly.
96PracticeDrawing clear diagramsCoreYour diagram is the artifact being judged.Illegible boxes and unlabelled arrows cost marks.
97PracticeHandling a topic you do not knowCoreReasoning from first principles beats bluffing.How you behave at the edge of your knowledge is assessed.
98PracticeJustifying every choice with a trade-offCore'I would use Redis' is incomplete. Why, and what does it cost?Every unjustified component invites a challenge.
99PracticeReading real engineering blogsCoreThe gap between textbook design and production reality.'Have you read how any real company solved this?'
